Privacy Policy (DRAFT — have a lawyer review before launch)

_Last updated: [date]_

Your numbers are yours. Private spots and private catches are visible only to you. We never make them public, and they are never used for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account: email address (for sign-in codes), optional display name and boat name. Real names are not required.
  • Location: your device location while the app is open, to show your position, speed and course. If you start a track or share your boat with a fishing group, location may also be used in the background until you stop.
  • Your content: saved spots, routes, tracks, catch logs, photos, posts and comments.
  • Technical data: crash reports and basic usage needed to keep the service working.
  • Anonymous usage stats (you can turn these off in Profile): a random ID created by the app (not your phone's hardware ID, not linked to your name or email), which screens and features are used (e.g. "turned on the water temperature layer"), app version, phone type, and a general area such as "Orange County" or "Offshore". We never collect your coordinates, spots, catches, notes, name or email for these stats. We use them to improve the app and to show sponsors overall audience numbers (for example, "5,000 monthly anglers, 40% in Orange County"). Raw usage events are deleted after 400 days.

How locations are shared

  • Saved spots, routes and tracks: stored on your phone; with an account, backed up privately. Only you can read them.
  • Catch reports: you choose per catch — Private, Area only, Approximate (moved 0.5–1.5 NM) or Exact — and can post anonymously. For Approximate and Area only, your exact position is never sent to other users.
  • Fishing groups: only to members of groups you joined, and only when you choose: turning sharing on (it ends automatically), sending your position or "Come to me", starting a float plan, or sharing a catch, spot or route with the group. Only your latest boat position is stored, not a history. After you stop sharing, your last position stays visible to your group for up to 24 hours unless you turn "Show my last known position" off or remove it.
  • Float plans: your back-by time, note and check-ins go to the groups you pick. If you don't check in, those members are alerted with your last shared position. A float plan is not an emergency service.
  • Group invites: the email addresses you invite are stored so the invite can be shown to that person when they sign in; other members see them partly hidden (e.g. j•••@gmail.com). Withdrawn or answered invites stop showing.
  • Photos: GPS and camera details are removed from photos before upload.

Advertising

Ads are matched to what's on screen (for example, the fish you're viewing) and to the general area your phone is in (for example "Orange County"), never to your precise location or private data. When you see or tap an ad, the app records an anonymous count (view, tap, call, directions, coupon saved, video played) with a device code that changes every day. Sponsors receive only totals (for example views, taps and general areas) — never information about you. Coupons you save stay on your phone.

Advertisers (Ad Studio)

Businesses that advertise give us their business name, contact details and the ads they upload. Ad files are stored publicly so the app can show them. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store card numbers.

Data sources

Charts, satellite, weather and tide data come from public government sources (mainly NOAA and the National Weather Service). NOAA does not endorse this app.

Your choices

You can delete catches, spots and posts at any time, and delete your whole account in the app under Me → Delete my account (this removes your profile, catches, photos, cloud spots, groups you own and posts). Contact: [support email].

We don't sell your data and don't track you across other apps or websites.
