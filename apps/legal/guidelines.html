Community Guidelines (DRAFT — review before launch)

This is a place for fishermen to share what's biting and help each other. Keep it that way.

Do

  • Post real catches, real reports and honest conditions.
  • Respect other people's spots. Approximate or area-only locations are always fine.
  • Disagree respectfully.

Don't

  • Post fake or misleading catch reports, or reports from places you weren't.
  • Post anyone else's private numbers, waypoints or personal information.
  • Harass, threaten, or use hate speech.
  • Spam, advertise without a sponsorship agreement, or sell illegal catch.
  • Post catches that break fishing regulations (closed seasons, MPAs, size or bag limits).

What happens

  • Anyone can report a post, comment or catch. After several independent reports, it's hidden until a moderator reviews it.
  • Moderators may remove content and suspend or ban accounts. Every moderation action is logged.
  • You can block or mute anyone; you won't see their posts or comments.
